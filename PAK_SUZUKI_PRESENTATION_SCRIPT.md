## **Executive Q&A Quick Reference**

- **Q: How do we track showroom footfall without invading customer privacy?**
  - **A:** *We use ceiling-mounted optical depth/LiDAR sensors that count anonymous silhouettes and dwell times around display cars. No facial recognition or personal data is collected, fully complying with PECA regulations.*
- **Q: Can this connect with our existing SAP/DMS?**
  - **A:** *Yes. The system uses standard REST/JSON APIs and webhooks to synchronize bookings, inventory, and customer deliveries with existing SAP or legacy dealership DMS instances.*
- **Q: How are 130-point inspections performed at expos?**
  - **A:** *Inspectors use mobile tablets linked to wireless OBD-II scanners to record engine compression, ECU codes, body weld inspections, and paint thickness, instantly generating a certified QR token.*
- **Q: What is the training timeline for dealership staff?**
  - **A:** *The interface is designed for consumer-grade simplicity. Sales consultants and service advisors require only a 30-minute orientation.*
- **Q: How is access and operational data secured?**
  - **A:** *All sessions are cryptographically signed with HMAC-SHA256, with role-based access control and TLS encryption in transit.*
